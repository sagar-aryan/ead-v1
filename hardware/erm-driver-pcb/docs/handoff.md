# Project Handoff

## Project
EAS V1 six-channel ERM haptic driver PCB (KiCad 10), built from `EAS_ERM_Driver_PCB_Handoff/`.

## Current Objective
Fabricate the V1 board by toner transfer, assemble it, and bring it up per handoff `07_VALIDATION_AND_TEST.md`.

## Variants
Four layouts of the same circuit, all with 0 jumpers and all verified in software:
- `EAS_ERM_Driver/`: single row, 82.75 × 19.0 mm.
- `EAS_ERM_Driver_compact/`: two rows, 44.70 × 32.60 mm (DEC-016).
- `EAS_ERM_Driver_mini/`: two rows with relaxed rules and standard lands, 38.91 × 29.28 mm (DEC-017).
- `EAS_ERM_Driver_tight/`: the mini with 0.3 mm gaps and tight courtyards, 36.07 × 27.60 mm (DEC-018).

The user chooses which to fabricate. Everything below applies to both unless stated.

## Current State
- **What works (verified in software):**
  - Complete schematic and single-layer PCB; ERC 0, DRC 0, 0 unconnected, schematic parity 0.
  - `check.py` passes every handoff verification item.
  - 82.75 × 19.0 mm; 0 vias, 0 holes, 0 jumpers.
  - Gerbers, a 1:1 mirrored toner PDF, assembly drawings and the BOM are in `EAS_ERM_Driver/output/`.
- **Not yet done:** paper fit check (TEST-007) and all hardware tests (TEST-008).

## Architecture
SWITCHED_SYSTEM+ feeds two HT7833 LDOs. Each makes its own 3.3 V rail for three low-side IRLML6344 channels (flyback diode, 10 nF across the motor, 100 nF local decoupling). Everything is wired off-board through 18 SMD pads. Details: `architecture.md`.

## Important Files
- `EAS_ERM_Driver/scripts/build.py`: **source of truth** (netlist model, schematic generator, layout).
- `EAS_ERM_Driver_compact/`: compact variant; same structure, its own `scripts/build.py` (layout section differs).
- `EAS_ERM_Driver_mini/`: mini variant; its own `scripts/build.py` (footprints, rules and layout differ; DEC-017).
- `EAS_ERM_Driver_tight/`: **the variant being fabricated** (DEC-019); its own `scripts/build.py`, plus `scripts/panel.py`, which tiles the verified board into the six-up toner sheet with its ruler (DEC-020) and verifies its own PDF.
- `EAS_ERM_Driver/scripts/check.py`: verification asserts and connection summary.
- `EAS_ERM_Driver/scripts/export.sh`: rebuild, verify and export everything.
- `EAS_ERM_Driver/EAS_ERM_Driver.kicad_{pro,sch,pcb,dru}`: generated KiCad project.
- `EAS_ERM_Driver/EAS.pretty/`, `EAS.kicad_sym`: generated project libraries.
- `EAS_ERM_Driver/output/`: deliverables and reports.
- `docs/`: this documentation.

## Known Problems
None open. Unverified items:
- Physical fit of the 220 µF V-chip and trimmed SOT-89 lands (TEST-007).
- Regulator temperature.
- LDO stability with the very-low-ESR 220 µF polymer.

## Failed Approaches
- **Grouped wire pads on the board edge with zero jumpers:** impossible (K3,3, PROB-002). Don't retry without accepting jumpers.
- **Stock KiCad SOT-89 land:** 0.254 mm internal gap violates the 0.4 mm rule (DEC-010).
- **GND pour under parts:** slivers and hidden bridge risk (DEC-011).

## Important Decisions
- **DEC-001:** 100 nF parts are per-channel local decoupling.
- **DEC-003:** PWM and M- pads are enclosed by copper.
- **DEC-008:** everything is generated by script.
- **DEC-013:** single-row floorplan.
- **DEC-019:** the tight variant is the one being fabricated, at 0.3 mm gaps.
- **DEC-020:** six copies per toner sheet, with a printed ruler as the scale check.

## Environment
Linux, KiCad 10.0.6 with the `pcbnew` Python module, Python 3.12, poppler-utils (`pdftoppm`), Pillow.

## How To Run
```bash
cd "EAS_ERM_Driver" && bash scripts/export.sh
```

## How To Verify
`export.sh` exits non-zero on any ERC error, DRC, unconnected or parity issue, or `check.py` failure. On success, `output/check.txt` ends with `ALL CHECKS PASSED`.

## Next Steps
0. Choose the variant to fabricate (single row, compact, mini or tight).
1. TEST-007 paper fit check. If a land is wrong, fix it in `build.py` and re-run `export.sh`.
2. Print the toner PDF at 100 %. For the tight variant use the six-up sheet `EAS_ERM_Driver_tight/output/EAS_ERM_Driver_tight_TONER_PANEL_6up_MIRRORED_print_1to1.pdf` (DEC-020): measure its ruler (tick 0 to tick 100 = 100.0 mm) to prove the printer did not rescale, cut the ruler strip off, then transfer, etch, cut and inspect (`hardware.md`).
3. Assemble with the 3:1 assembly drawing; bring up per handoff 07 (TEST-008) and record the results in `testing.md`.
4. Optional V1.1: conformal coat; review the regulator temperature data.

## Warnings
- **Do not hand-edit the KiCad files and then re-run `build.py`:** the edits are overwritten.
- **Never join HAPTIC_3V3_A and HAPTIC_3V3_B.**
- **Never feed raw BAT into PWR+.** The master switch must sit between the charger LOAD+ output and PWR+.
- **`ESP+` is the switched battery voltage, not 3.3 V.** Connect it to the XIAO battery input only.
- **Files are KiCad 10 format** (unreadable in KiCad ≤ 9).
- **The board is bare copper** (no solder mask): watch for bridges near the 0.4 mm gaps.
